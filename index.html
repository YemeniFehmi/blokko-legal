Blokko is a block puzzle game developed by Fehmi Yemeni. Blokko does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

2. Data We Collect

Blokko does not collect any personal information:

Blokko does not integrate any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics tools.

Blokko is suitable for all ages. We do not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
